Frequently asked questions

How do I keep track of wines I liked?
Write it down while the glass is still in front of you. Swirl makes that a 30-second habit: snap the label, rate, jot a line. Next store trip, your own top-rated list does the choosing.

What should I write in a wine tasting journal?
Name and producer, vintage, where you drank it, a rating, and a few honest words. The label photo covers everything you'd otherwise mistype.

Do I need an account or internet?
No — Swirl is fully offline. No account, no cloud, no tracking.
